Delete Your Reebaby Account

Reebaby is developed by Chillbaby Technologies. You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data using the steps below.

Method 1 — Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Reebaby app on your phone.
  2. Log in with your registered email and password.
  3. Tap the menu and go to Settings.
  4. Tap My Account.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Confirm the action when prompted.

Your account and data are removed immediately after confirmation.

Method 2 — Email request (if you cannot access the app)

Send an email to: support@chillbabytechnologies.com

Subject: Delete My Reebaby Account

Include in the email:

  • Your registered full name
  • Your registered email address
  • Your registered phone number

We will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Account profile: name, email, phone number, country, language
  • Child profile information
  • Paired Bluetooth (BA4) device records and pairing history
  • Chat messages with support
  • Notification tokens
  • App preferences and settings
  • Uploaded profile photo or avatar

What data is retained

  • Anonymized crash logs and diagnostic data (retained up to 90 days for service quality)
  • Records required by law, fraud prevention, or financial compliance (retained for the period required by applicable law)

Retention period

All personal data is permanently removed within 30 days of the request. Backup copies are purged within 90 days.
