Delete Your Reebaby Account

We respect your right to control your personal data. Choose one of the options below to permanently remove your account and associated information.

1 Delete from inside the app Recommended

  1. Open the Reebaby app on your phone.
  2. Log in with your registered email and password.
  3. Tap the menu and go to Settings.
  4. Tap My Account.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Confirm the action when prompted.

Your account and data will be removed immediately after confirmation.

2 Email request

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request by email:

Send to
support@chillbabytechnologies.com
Subject: Delete My Reebaby Account
Include: registered name, email, and phone number.

We verify your identity and process the request within 30 days.

i What happens to your data

Deleted

  • Profile (name, email, phone)
  • Country & language
  • Child profile information
  • Paired BA4 device records
  • Chat messages
  • Notification tokens
  • App preferences
  • Profile photo

Retained

  • Anonymized crash logs (90 days)
  • Legal compliance records
  • Fraud-prevention records
⏱
Retention timeline Personal data permanently removed within 30 days. Backups purged within 90 days.
