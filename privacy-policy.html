Privacy Policy

How Reebaby collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. Read this policy carefully to understand our practices.

Effective: 2026-06-08

This Privacy Policy explains how Chillbaby Technologies ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Reebaby mobile application ("the App") and the paired Reebaby BA4 Bluetooth hardware device.

1Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account info: full name, email address, password (stored hashed), phone number, country, preferred language.
  • Child profile info: child's name, age, gender, and any other details you add for monitoring purposes.
  • Profile photo / avatar: optional image you upload.
  • Support chat messages: messages you send to our support team.

Information collected automatically

  • Device identifiers: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token, device model, OS version.
  • Bluetooth device data: paired BA4 device MAC address, name, pairing history, telemetry (status, battery, alerts).
  • Location data: approximate location only if you grant Bluetooth scan permission on Android (required by the operating system to perform BLE scans). We do not track real-time GPS location.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and error reports (anonymized).
  • App usage events: basic interaction events (screen visits, feature usage).

2How we use information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Verify your identity via SMS one-time password (OTP).
  • Pair and operate the Reebaby BA4 hardware device.
  • Deliver alerts and push notifications.
  • Provide customer support via in-app chat and email.
  • Improve app stability and fix bugs.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following categories of service providers, strictly for the purposes listed:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): push notification delivery (FCM), crash reporting, analytics.
  • Cloud hosting providers: storing account data and backups.
  • SMS gateway providers: delivering OTP verification and emergency SMS.
  • Legal authorities: when required by law, court order, or legal process.

5Data security

  • All network traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS).
  • Passwords are stored in hashed form on our servers.
  • Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

6Data retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days. Backups are purged within 90 days.
  • Anonymized crash and diagnostic logs may be retained up to 90 days.
  • Records required by law (e.g., financial or fraud-prevention) are retained for the period required by applicable law.

7Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (see Delete Account).
  • Withdraw consent for processing where applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any right, contact us at support@chillbabytechnologies.com.

8Children's privacy

The Reebaby app is intended for use by adults (parents or guardians) aged 18 and over. The app monitors hardware paired with a child but is not used directly by children. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

9International data transfers

Data may be processed and stored in countries other than your country of residence. We rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g., standard contractual clauses) where required.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. Material changes will be notified in the app or by email.
